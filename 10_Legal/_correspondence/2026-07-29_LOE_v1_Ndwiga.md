Letter of Engagement between Mary Thuo (hereinafter referred to as the "Client") and Ndwiga Law Advocates LLP ("we" or "us").

## 1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 We understand that you wish to appoint us as your advocates to incorporate a company through which you will operate your software/agency business (the "Company"), to protect the intellectual property associated with it, and to put in place the data protection registrations required for it to trade lawfully in Kenya.

1.2 We further understand that your priority is to reach the point at which the Company legally exists and can trade — incorporation, KRA PIN, and the ability to open a bank account — as quickly as possible, so that you can market and present the business publicly, while the trademark, copyright and data protection registrations proceed in parallel rather than gating that launch. We have structured the Services and fees below accordingly, in two phases.

1.3 This Letter does not cover the intended holding company, the group structure above the Company, or the ownership and licensing of intellectual property between a holding company and the Company. You have indicated that you are assembling the information for that separate entity, and we will advise on it, and on the correct sequencing of incorporation between the two entities, once that information is to hand and under a separate letter of engagement or addendum.

1.4 This Letter also does not cover the eight matters raised in your operational brief (cross-border data transfers and standard contractual clauses, a sub-processor register, USD/KES contract terms, appointment of a named AI-governance reviewer, and review of your seven draft contract templates). We address which of these, if any, fall within the fee below at clause 2.3, and will provide a separate estimate for the remainder.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 We understand that under these Terms of Engagement, we are undertaking to provide the following Services, structured in two phases:

### Phase 1 – Formation and Trading Readiness (priority)

2.1.1 Advise on, and incorporate, the Company with two classes of shares (Class A and Class B, comprising Ordinary and Preference shares), all of which will be held by you as sole shareholder on incorporation. This preserves your sole ownership and control now while creating the structure needed to allot or transfer Class B shares to future partners later, without a further restructuring;

2.1.2 Advise on, and size, the nominal share capital and its allocation between the two classes, having regard to the stamp duty payable on nominal share capital, so that this is fixed deliberately rather than by default;

2.1.3 Obtain a KRA PIN for the Company;

2.1.4 Advise on what you may lawfully do to market and present the software/agency online before incorporation is complete, and what must appear on any website or promotional material, and in what name, during that interim period;

2.1.5 Keep You updated by way of written communication of any development in the matter within 48 hours of such attendance or notice of any development.

### Phase 2 – Intellectual Property Protection and Regulatory Registration (running in parallel, in the background)

2.1.6 Advise on what copyright subsists in AI-assisted work, who is treated as the author, what records evidence sufficient human authorship, and whether registration with the Kenya Copyright Board (KECOBO) is worthwhile given that copyright arises automatically and registration is evidentiary rather than constitutive. This advice will have regard to the reasoning in *Aryeh Movement Limited v. Cynthia Beldina Akoth Okello* (COPTA/E001/2025) on the human-authorship requirement, and to the version-controlled record of direction, specification and revision that you have indicated you can produce;

2.1.7 Proceed with copyright registration at KECOBO if, following that advice, you instruct us to do so;

2.1.8 Register the Company's trademark(s) at the Kenya Industrial Property Institute (IPOK);

2.1.9 Advise on, and attend to, the Company's registration as a data controller and data processor with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

2.2 This Letter of Engagement sets out the basis on which we will provide the Services to You. Your continued communication with us will be taken to indicate your acceptance of these Terms of Engagement, but for the sake of good order please print a copy of this letter, sign it in the space provided below, and email it back to us as a PDF.

2.3 The fee at clause 4 covers the nine items listed at clauses 2.1.1 to 2.1.9 above only. It does not cover the holding company work referred to at clause 1.3, or the eight matters referred to at clause 1.4 (cross-border transfer advice and standard contractual clauses, the sub-processor register, USD/KES contract terms, appointment of a named AI-governance reviewer, and review of your seven draft contract templates), all of which fall outside this scope. Once you confirm you wish to proceed with that further work, we will provide a separate written estimate of the marginal fee for it, as we do not provide fee estimates orally.

2.4 The Letter of Engagement will apply in relation to any instructions from you and, unless otherwise agreed and subject to our remuneration rates, any other matters upon which you may instruct us in the future.

2.5 We would be pleased to discuss the provision of additional services to you beyond the scope of the Services set out above. However, unless we expressly agree in writing to provide additional services, please be advised that the Terms of Engagement relate only to the Services set out above.

## 3. PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY

3.1 Sarah Ndwiga, Managing Partner, is your point of contact for any queries relating to this engagement and provision of the Services. Collins Wachira will assist in the conduct of this matter and in issuing this Letter of Engagement to you. We will always try to discuss any change in personnel with you in advance, but occasionally it may be necessary to introduce other third-party professionals without consulting you so as to ensure that urgent deadlines are met or absences by team members are adequately covered. We may also assign tasks to junior colleagues where it is cost-effective to do so.

3.2 We will inform you whenever this is necessary and provide the necessary contact details.

## 4. FEES AND EXPENSES PAYABLE

4.1 Given the scope of work set out in Section 2, we shall charge an all-inclusive legal fee of Kenya Shillings One Hundred and Eighty Thousand (Kshs. 180,000), exclusive of the government and official charges referred to at clauses 4.2 and 4.3 below.

4.2 The breakdown of our legal fee is as follows:

| DESCRIPTION | LEGAL FEE (Kshs.) |
|---|---|
| 1. Company incorporation, including KRA PIN (Phase 1) | 55,000 |
| 2. Copyright advice and registration at KECOBO — government charge of Kshs. 5,000 applies (Phase 2) | 35,000 |
| 3. Trademark registration at IPOK — exclusive of IPOK official fees (Phase 2) | 70,000 |
| 4. Data controller and data processor registration — exclusive of ODPC official fees (Phase 2) | 25,000 |
| **TOTAL LEGAL FEE** | **180,000** |

4.3 In addition to the legal fee above, government and official charges will apply. The KECOBO copyright filing charge of Kshs. 5,000 is fixed. The stamp duty and e-Citizen charges on incorporation, and the IPOK and ODPC official fees, depend on the nominal share capital and the number/classes of trademark and data-processing categories registered; we will confirm these in writing once clauses 2.1.1, 2.1.2 and 2.1.8 above are settled with you, and add them to the relevant invoice as disbursements rather than estimate them now.

## 5. INSTALMENTS AND PHASING

5.1 We understand that the Company is pre-revenue, that you are funding this engagement personally, and that predictability of cash flow matters more to you than speed. We have therefore structured payment by phase and by work-stream, rather than as a single upfront fee, as follows:

### Phase 1 – Formation and Trading Readiness (Kshs. 55,000)

5.2 Fifty percent (Kshs. 27,500) is payable on your signing and returning this Letter of Engagement, to enable us to commence work; and the balance (Kshs. 27,500), together with any incorporation-related government charges then confirmed, is payable on issue of the Certificate of Incorporation and KRA PIN.

### Phase 2 – Intellectual Property Protection and Regulatory Registration (Kshs. 125,000, exclusive of official fees)

5.3 Each Phase 2 work-stream is invoiced separately, when you instruct us to commence it, so that this work can proceed in the background at a pace that suits you and need not be paid for as a single lump sum:

5.3.1 Copyright advice and registration — Kshs. 35,000 plus the Kshs. 5,000 KECOBO charge, invoiced when you instruct us to file;
5.3.2 Trademark registration — Kshs. 70,000 plus IPOK official fees (to be confirmed), invoiced on commencement of the IPOK application;
5.3.3 Data controller/processor registration — Kshs. 25,000 plus ODPC official fees (to be confirmed), invoiced on commencement of the ODPC application.

5.4 Where any withholding or deduction of tax is required by law, the amount of the payment due shall be increased to an amount which (after making the relevant withholding or deduction) leaves an amount equal to the payment which would have been due if no such withholding or deduction had been required.

5.5 We reserve the right to increase our fees on a case-by-case basis with your prior written agreement.

5.6 We will also charge to you any disbursements and other expenses we incur on your behalf. VAT will be charged to you in addition where applicable on fees, disbursements and expenses.

The engagement will commence on the date of this Letter of Engagement and will continue until completion of delivery of the Services, or our relationship is terminated.
